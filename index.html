Optional: add Google Safe Browsing

Without a key, the scanner uses local checks only. If you have your own Google Safe Browsing API key, you can add it to also check the link against Google's threat lists. The key is stored only in this browser, and the link is sent to Google when you check. Use a key restricted to your site's domain, and never paste a key you cannot afford to expose.
